Who is Bijou?

Bijou Nicole is an R&B/Soul artist whose catalog moves between intimacy, confidence, nostalgia and experimentation. Each project is treated like its own world — sound, visuals, styling and story moving together.

Her site is built the same way: not around one release, but around the full artist universe.
