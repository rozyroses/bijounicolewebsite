About Bijou

Music for every version of you.

Bijou Nicole is an R&B/Soul artist whose work moves between romance, healing, fantasy, family and self-discovery. Her public catalog stretches from intimate early projects to collaborative work with The Jewelettes and the cinematic world of The Life of a Daydreamer.

Her music is released through Wolfpark Recordings and the broader Prism Nine Entertainment universe.
