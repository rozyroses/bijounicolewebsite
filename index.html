About Bijou

Soft edges. Big worlds.

Bijou Nicole is an AI music artist developed as a fully realized creative universe — singer, songwriter, performer and visual storyteller. Her work blends R&B, pop and cinematic world-building with an evolving digital identity.

Presented through Wolfpark Recordings under Prism Nine Entertainment, Bijou's releases connect music, imagery, performance concepts and narrative into one continuous story.
